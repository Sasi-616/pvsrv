How To Use This Admin Panel

A simple 1-minute visual guide for anyone to manage the website

📝 Publishing Articles & Insights

Go to Articles tab. Click '+ New Article', enter your title and body text, pick a category, and click 'Publish to Website'.

📚 Books & 3D Flipbook Reader

Go to Books Studio. Click '+ Add Book Title' (or use the sample button), specify chapters, and preview realistic page turns in 3D right inside your browser!

🎯 Interactive Knowledge Quiz

Go to Quiz Platform. Click '+ Add Question', enter options and correct answer with a brief explanation. Users can test their knowledge on the public quiz page.

🛡️ 1-Click Backup & Recovery

Never worry about losing work! Click 'Backup' to download everything to your computer. Click 'Restore' anytime to bring back saved content.

✨ Pro-Tip: Whenever you open a form, look for the green '✨ Fill Sample' button. It will populate realistic sample data so you can test immediately with zero effort!
